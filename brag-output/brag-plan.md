# Brag Plan: tryoncam

## What is this app?
A Chrome extension that puts clothes from any online store on you, live, through your own camera, before you buy.

## The angle
Everyone knows the order → hate it → return loop. tryoncam breaks it in one click. Fast kinetic type carries the voiceover; the product itself (Try On pills, the dark trial-room panel, live swaps) is the centerpiece.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Three words slam in on the beat of the voice: "Ordered it." / "Hated it." / "Sent it back." The last one gets stamped "AGAIN?"

## Key moments
- Try On pills pop onto real product photos (Heritage, true love club, coral shirts), cursor clicks one.
- The trial room panel snaps open: live view, 0:15 countdown, "3 sessions" chip.
- Rapid-fire garment swaps with kinetic "Swap. Swap. Swap." type.

## Outro / punchline
Logo mark bounces in, "tryoncam", "3 free try-ons", "Add to Chrome — it's free", tryoncam.com.

## User flow worth showing
Browse a store → hover and click Try On → see it on you live → swap to another garment without restarting.

## Tone
- Preset: default (pushed toward chaotic pacing)
- Creative direction: fast-paced kinetic launch spot
- Interpretation: hard cuts and slam-in type, 6 scenes, but every line holds long enough to read; warm confident voice, no corporate language.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #f6f5f1 (cream), dark scenes #0e0f12
- Accent: #3d5bf5 (blue), #8b7cf6 (violet); panel green pulse #22c55e
- Text: #0e0f12
- Display font: Bricolage Grotesque
- Body font: Geist / Geist Mono
- Strongest visual element: the black "Try On" pill with its travelling spark, and the dark trial-room panel

## Share copy (draft)
Stop ordering clothes you'll return. tryoncam puts them on you, live, on any store — 3 free try-ons.

## Audio direction
- Role: voiceover-led, with motion-matched SFX
- Music: none (user request)
- Voice: Kokoro via Hyperframes, warm and upbeat, conversational
- SFX posture: moderate: whooshes on cuts, a click on Try On, soft pops on pills, a stamp on "AGAIN?", a hit on the logo
- Restraint rule: SFX never cover the voice; no risers, no music beds

## Voiceover script (~52 words, ~22s)
| # | Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | 0.0–3.0 | "Ordered it." / "Hated it." / "Sent it back." + stamp "AGAIN?" | "Ordered it. Hated it. Sent it back… again?" |
| 2 Reveal | 3.0–6.0 | Logo slams in: **tryoncam** · "See it on you before you buy it." | "Meet tryoncam. See it on you before you buy it." |
| 3 Try On | 6.0–10.0 | Store grid; Try On pills pop one by one; cursor clicks. Type: "Hover. Click Try On." | "Browse any store, and hit Try On on anything you like." |
| 4 Live | 10.0–14.0 | Trial-room panel snaps open, live view, 0:15 countdown. Type: "Live. On you." | "There it is — on you, live, through your own camera." |
| 5 Swap | 14.0–18.0 | Three quick garment swaps. Type: "Swap." "Swap." "Swap." then "No restart." | "Not feeling it? Swap. Swap again. No restart." |
| 6 Outro | 18.0–22.0 | Logo mark, **tryoncam**, "3 free try-ons", "Add to Chrome — it's free", tryoncam.com | "tryoncam. Three free try-ons. Add it to Chrome." |

## Storyboard notes
Sequential/interaction: pills pop one by one (scene 3), simulated cursor click (scene 3), panel open (scene 4), swap sequence (scene 5).
Transitions: hard cuts and quick zoom-punches between scenes; whip-slide into the outro.
Stand-in note: the "live" try-on uses the site's model photos (m-heritage, m-coral, m-love) with slight movement, not a recorded session.
