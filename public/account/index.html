      Frequently asked

      Credits & sessions

      What is a credit?

      One credit starts one live try-on session. Inside a session you can swap between as many garments as you like, for as long as the session runs.

      Do credits expire?

      No. Credits you buy stay in your account for as long as it is open.

      A session failed to start. Did I lose a credit?

      No. A credit is used only once the live video starts. A session that never starts gives its credit back automatically.

      Payments

      I paid but my credits are not showing

      They usually arrive within a few seconds. Refresh this page; if they are still missing after a few minutes, email us with the date of the payment and we will sort it out.

      Where are my receipts?

      Open Billing and choose Receipts & payment methods. Stripe, our payment processor, keeps every receipt there.

      Can I get a refund?

      Purchases are final, as set out in our refund policy. The free credits you get on sign-in are there to try it first.

      Using tryoncam

      Which shops does it work on?

      Most online clothing stores. Hover a product photo and a Try On button appears. Tops, dresses, outerwear and bottoms work best. Hats work too; jewellery, shoes and bags are not supported yet.

      Is my camera video saved?

      No. Your camera is on only while a session runs, and the video is processed in real time, never recorded or stored by us. Our privacy policy has the details.

      How do I delete my account?

      Go to Settings and choose Request deletion, or email us. Unused credits are forfeited when an account is deleted.
